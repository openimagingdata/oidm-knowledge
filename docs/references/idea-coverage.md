Reference inventory, consolidated 2026-10-02 from the September 22 investigation. This is coverage evidence, not a task queue. Current work and presentation destinations live in the [implementation plan](../plans/implementation.md).

The 87 ideas retain their `Sem-` and `Use-` IDs. The linked historical entries contain pinned repository evidence, related ideas, and qualifications. Their source dates and proposal status remain significant; they do not establish present-day implementation or project-wide agreement.

Corrections from the completed source-fidelity reviews govern reuse:

- Use-A5: derived status can be stored. Viewer v2 stores `viewerMetadata.statusByFindingId`; preserve each source's vocabulary.
- Use-B5: keep the webinar's families distinct from manuscript groupings. Quality surveillance and research are separate webinar families; breast imaging is a separate planned example.
- Use-B9: boards describe proposed workflows. Cite the corrected Sample Applications draft's public team evidence for demonstrations that actually occurred.
- Use-A20: the formal-model SDK is proposed. Do not call the extraction platform its precursor without supporting evidence.
- Use-A7 belongs primarily with Observation provenance; IPL corrections and merge/split behavior stay with the IPL account. Use-A13, A14, and A22 remain with IPL.
- Use-A17 and A26 belong with Exam Finding List; Sem-B8 and B9 belong with OIFM content; Sem-C6 and C7 describe the finding-model SDK's local practices.

The historical inventories contain material unsuitable for direct publication, including quoted manuscript passages.
